HW 6 Texture Mapping

Edit the shaders on the left; the canvas updates as you type.
Drag with the mouse to rotate the models.
Use the arrow keys and w, s keys to move the camera.
Use the slider and dropdown to rotate about an axis; the button starts/stops spinning.

A snowman holding a snow globe.
Sphere: snow globe with a video texture, body and head with bump mapping, and a huge one around the scene for the night sky.
Torus: ring under the globe with normal mapping.
Cone: carrot nose with bump mapping.
From the skeleton: cube (platform), cylinder (top hat, arms, button eyes).
